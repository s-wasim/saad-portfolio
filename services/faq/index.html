Before you book

How much does a website for a small business cost?

With me, a one-page site starts at $200. Larger business sites, Shopify stores and custom stores cost more; you get one fixed price after a free 20-minute audit, before any work starts.

How much does an AI automation for a small business cost?

A single automation starts at $400, for example turning inquiries into drafted quotes. A bundle of three, or a custom AI agent for a bigger workflow, is quoted as one fixed price after the free audit.

How much does it cost to set up an AI assistant like Claude for my business?

A Claude for Small Business setup starts at $250 for one person: connected to the tools you already use, a few workflows tuned to your work, and a walkthrough. Team setups for 2 to 10 people are quoted after the audit.

Will an AI send messages to my customers without me?

No. Everything I build drafts first and waits: nothing goes to a customer until a person presses Approve.

Who owns the website, the data and the accounts?

You do. Software subscriptions are in your name and paid by you directly, systems run on accounts or machines you own, and you keep everything if we stop working together.

How do payments work?

A fixed quote, 50% up front and 50% on delivery, after a 7-day review against the agreed scope.

What happens after it is delivered?

Two months of support are included: fixes and small changes, up to 5 hours a month. After that, $30 an hour if you need me.

Do you work with businesses in the US?

Yes. I am based in Lahore, Pakistan and work remotely with businesses in the US and worldwide; calls are booked for US mornings, which are my evenings.
