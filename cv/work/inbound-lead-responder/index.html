inbound_lead_responder

Six-node email agent. Classifies, extracts, routes on a rules table, drafts a proposal — then hands it to the right team. Nothing goes back to the lead without a person.

$ lead-agent --watch inbox
  1. [classify] inbound → NEW_LEAD 0.7s
  2. [extract] lead fields · 9 keys
  3. [route] rules table → enterprise
  4. [draft] proposal_acme.docx 1.9s
  5. ✓ handover sent · 4 claude calls

What it does

An email agent that reads inbound messages, sorts real leads from spam and support, extracts the lead's details, routes it to the right team using an editable rules table, and drafts a proposal document — then hands it to that team in an internal email, so a person decides what goes back to the lead.

How it works

  • A six-node LangGraph graph: it classifies the email, extracts the lead, routes it, drafts the proposal and hands it over.
  • Routing follows a keyword rules table the team can edit; Claude only breaks ties.
  • Proposals are generated as Word documents with python-docx.
  • At most four Claude calls per lead email.
  • Every run has a node-by-node trace, so you can see why a lead went where it did.

By the numbers

  • 6 nodes
  • ≤4 Claude calls per lead
  • 0 emails sent to the lead automatically
